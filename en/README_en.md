# Doctrina — The Doctrine of Systems Design

## On the Stratification of the Doctrine

The works of the Doctrine are arranged by an expanding horizon of thought. **Principium** and **Anti-Icarus** are for anyone beginning the path of long-term creation. **Absurdum ad Veritatem** is a tool for the mature architect. **Shadow Co-Author**, **Dignified Decommissioning**, and **Compatibility Lexicon** are upper-level principles intended for systems that have already lived for decades. It is not necessary to understand them all today. It is enough to know that they exist and to return to them when the system has grown.
